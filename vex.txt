# Outline for GanjaGuru: AI-Super Powered Virtual Budtender

## I. Concept Overview

- **Definition**: An advanced AI-driven virtual assistant designed to replicate and enhance the role of a traditional budtender, offering comprehensive advice and services related to cannabis products, cultivation, and business management.
- **Core Functions**:
  - Acts as a budtender, cannabis consultant, business coach, software and data engineer.
  - Searches for potential buyers and procures products at the best cost.
  - Performs dynamic pricing and marketing, completing sales, packaging, and delivery.

## II. Brand Voice and Visual Identity

### A. Brand Voice

- **Personality Mix**: Inspired by Willie Nelson, Snoop Dogg, Bruce Lee, John Lennon, Paul McCartney, and the swagger of a college kid.
- **Tone Attributes**:
  - Laid-back confidence with deep wisdom.
  - Zen-like philosophy with a focus on personal growth.
  - Revolutionary thinking with a touch of youthful swagger.

### B. Visual Identity

- **Color Palette**: 
  - Blend of marijuana-inspired colors: greens, yellows, purples with metallics (silver and gold).
- **Typography**:
  - Retro-futuristic fonts combining Roboto, Futura, and others for a blend of style and readability.
- **Button Text**: Use playful marijuana phrases for CTA buttons like “Take a Toke”.

## III. Functional Features

### A. User Onboarding

- Engaging welcome flow to capture preferences and goals.

### B. Dynamic Knowledge Retrieval

- **The GanjaGuru**: Provides cannabis advice, product guidance, and tips.
- **PotGrowHub**: Focuses on product showcasing and IoT solution integration.

### C. Interactive Engagement

- Natural, multi-turn interactions maintained with memory nodes for continuity.

### D. Recommendations and Guidance

- Product search and grow room design recommendations utilizing AR/VR.

### E. Gamified Experience

- Use of Budz currency to reward user actions and maintain engagement.

## IV. Chat Configuration

### A. Interface Settings

- Predefined welcome message and inline evaluations for transparency.
- Feedback and response ratings from users.

### B. Interaction Features

- Typing indicators and session context awareness.
- Quick actions for common queries.

### C. Design and Accessibility

- Marijuana-based color theme with accessibility options like text-to-speech.

### D. Integration and Multimedia

- API hooks for real-time product recommendations and multimedia support in the chat.
